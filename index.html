本页面是 Google OAuth 应用 rclone-liny 的开发者主页，用于满足 Google 的 OAuth 品牌信息要求。

This page is the developer home for the Google OAuth client rclone-liny, provided to satisfy Google's OAuth branding requirements.

这是什么 / What it is

rclone 是一个开源命令行工具。本 OAuth 客户端仅由开发者本人在自己的设备上使用，用于在自己的电脑与自己的 Google Drive 之间同步和备份文件。

rclone is an open-source command-line tool. This OAuth client is used solely by its developer, on their own devices, to sync and back up files between their own computer and their own Google Drive.

谁在使用 / Who uses it

仅开发者本人（单一用户）。不对外分发，不提供任何公开服务，无服务器端组件。

The developer only (a single user). It is not distributed, offers no public service, and has no server-side component.
